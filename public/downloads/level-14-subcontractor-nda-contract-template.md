### 2. CORE OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORK & TEMPLATE BLUEPRINT
### SUBCONTRACTOR WHITE-LABEL AGREEMENT

```text
1. NON-SOLICITATION & CLIENT CONFIDENTIALITY
Subcontractor agrees that during the term of this agreement and for a period of 2 years thereafter, Subcontractor shall not directly or indirectly contact, solicit, or perform services for any client of [Agency Name].

2. WHITE-LABEL REPRESENTATION
Subcontractor shall represent [Agency Name] when communicating in client workspaces (using @agency domain emails or assigned Slack handles).

3. PAYMENT TERMS
Subcontractor shall be compensated at the rate of $[Amount]/Hour, payable bi-weekly upon verification of Toggl time logs and quality approval.

SIGNATURES:
Agency Owner: ____________________ Date: _________
Subcontractor: ___________________ Date: _________
```
