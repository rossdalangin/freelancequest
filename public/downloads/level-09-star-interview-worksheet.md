### 2. CORE OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORK & TEMPLATE BLUEPRINT
### THE STAR METHOD WORKSHEET

Fill out your practice stories for common interview questions:

#### Story 1: Handling a Difficult Client / Tight Deadline
- **S - Situation:** Client needed a 500-lead spreadsheet formatted and verified in 12 hours due to an upcoming campaign launch.
- **T - Task:** Clean, verify, and format data while maintaining 98%+ deliverability accuracy.
- **A - Action:** Deployed Apollo.io filters, ran batch verification in NeverBounce, and organized Google Sheet using standard styling.
- **R - Result:** Delivered 500 verified leads 2 hours ahead of deadline with zero email bounces. Client extended retainer.

#### Practice Prompts Matrix
1. *Tell me about a time you made a mistake and how you resolved it.*
2. *How do you prioritize tasks when multiple clients send urgent requests?*
