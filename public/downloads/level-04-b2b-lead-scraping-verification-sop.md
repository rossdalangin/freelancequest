### 2. CORE OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORK & TEMPLATE BLUEPRINT
### B2B LEAD GENERATION & VERIFICATION BLUEPRINT

#### 1. Tool Stack & Scraper Setup
- Prospecting: Apollo.io / LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Email Lookup & Verification: NeverBounce / ZeroBounce / Hunter.io
- Formatting: Google Sheets / Excel

#### 2. Step-by-Step Lead Extraction SOP
1. **Target Search Filters:** Set ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) parameters:
   - Industry: e.g. B2B SaaS / Real Estate
   - Title: CEO, Founder, Head of Marketing
   - Location: US, Canada, UK
   - Company Size: 11-50 employees
2. **Data Scraping:** Extract Lead Name, Verified Email, Title, Company Name, LinkedIn URL, Company Website.
3. **Email Verification:** Run extracted emails through NeverBounce. Remove any emails classified as 'Invalid' or 'Catch-All'. Maintain >97% deliverability rate.
4. **Formatting Cleanliness:** Capitalize proper names, remove legal suffixes (Inc., LLC) from company names for personalized outreach.
