### 2. CORE OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORK & TEMPLATE BLUEPRINT
### INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR SERVICE AGREEMENT

```text
This Agreement is entered into on [Date] by and between [Your Name/Business] ("Contractor") and [Client Name/Company] ("Client").

1. SERVICES PROVIDED
Contractor shall perform Virtual Assistant and administrative services as outlined in Statement of Work (SOW).

2. COMPENSATION & PAYMENT TERMS
- Hourly Rate / Retainer: $[Amount] USD per [Hour/Month].
- Payment Schedule: Invoices billed bi-weekly / upfront monthly.
- Payment Terms: Due upon receipt (NET 0). Late payments subject to 5% weekly penalty.

3. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS
Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee. Contractor maintains autonomy over work location and tools.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & CONFIDENTIALITY
All work deliverables created for Client become exclusive property of Client upon full payment. Contractor agrees to non-disclosure of proprietary client data.

5. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement with 14 days written notice.

SIGNATURES:
Contractor: ____________________ Date: _________
Client: ________________________ Date: _________
```
