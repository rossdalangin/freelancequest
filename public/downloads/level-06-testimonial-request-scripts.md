### 2. CORE OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORK & TEMPLATE BLUEPRINT
### TESTIMONIAL COLLECTION PLAYBOOK

#### The 3 Golden Moments to Ask for a Testimonial
1. Immediately after delivering a high-value task or project ahead of deadline.
2. During a monthly ROI review call when the client praises your work.
3. Upon completing 30 days of seamless VA support.

#### 4 Testimonial Prompt Questions
1. *What was your biggest operational headache before we started working together?*
2. *How has my VA support impacted your daily workload or peace of mind?*
3. *What specific result or metric stood out most to you?*
4. *What would you say to another founder considering hiring me?*

### 3. COPY-PASTE CLIENT COMMUNICATION & DELIVERY SCRIPTS
#### Frictionless Testimonial Request Email Template
```text
Subject: Quick favor / 2-minute feedback on our work together!

Hi [Client Name],

It has been a pleasure supporting [Company Name] over the past month! I'm so glad we successfully established your inbox protocols and lead pipeline.

I am updating my portfolio with recent client experiences. Would you be open to leaving a brief 2-minute review?

To make it completely effortless, you can simply reply to this email answering 1 question:
"How has delegating administrative tasks saved you time this month?"

(Or if easier, you can approve this draft text: "[Your Name] is an absolute lifesaver! They handled our inbox and lead scraping flawlessly, saving me 10+ hours every week.")

Thank you so much for your trust and support!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```
