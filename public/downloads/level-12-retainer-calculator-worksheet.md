### 2. CORE OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORK & TEMPLATE BLUEPRINT
### FREELANCE RETAINER PRICING CALCULATOR

#### 1. Target Monthly Income Formula
```text
[Desired Net Monthly Income] + [30% Taxes & Software Overhead] = [Gross Revenue Target]
Example: $3,000 Net + $900 Overhead = $3,900 Gross Monthly Target

[Gross Monthly Target] / [Billable Hours per Month (e.g. 100 Hrs)] = Minimum Hourly Rate
Example: $3,900 / 100 Hours = $39.00 / Hour
```
