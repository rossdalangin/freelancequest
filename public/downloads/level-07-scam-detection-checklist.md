# Scam Detection & Client Vetting Checklist

## Red Flag Checks
- [ ] Client asks for check deposit and funds transfer before starting work (SCAM)
- [ ] Client requests off-platform chat transfers (Telegram/WhatsApp) before contract (DANGER)
- [ ] Client refuses to verify payment method or escrow

## Green Light Indicators
- [ ] Verified payment method on job board
- [ ] Positive review history from past freelancers
- [ ] Clear written job description and milestone terms
