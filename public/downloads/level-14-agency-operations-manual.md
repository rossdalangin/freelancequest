### 2. CORE OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORK & TEMPLATE BLUEPRINT
### VIRTUAL AGENCY OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORK

#### 1. Subcontractor Vetting & Recruitment Pipeline
- **Step 1:** Post detailed job scorecard outlining exact skills required.
- **Step 2:** Administer a paid 1-hour trial task (e.g. scrape 20 leads + format sheet).
- **Step 3:** Evaluate task on accuracy, speed, and communication quality.
- **Step 4:** Execute Subcontractor NDA and Independent Contractor Agreement.

#### 2. Subcontractor Quality Assurance Protocol
- All work submitted by junior VAs must pass a 10-point internal audit before client delivery.
- Maintain a margin ratio of 40-50% between client billing rate and subcontractor pay rate.
