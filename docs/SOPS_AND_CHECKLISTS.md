# Virtual Assistant Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) & Checklists

This document provides ready-to-use Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and checklists designed for Virtual Assistants, Executive Partners, and Remote Freelancers.

---

## SOP 1: Executive Calendar & Multi-Timezone Scheduling

### Objective
Manage executive calendars flawlessly, preventing double bookings and time zone confusion.

### Procedure
1. **Timezone Reference Setup:**
   - Always verify the client's primary timezone (e.g. EST, PST, GMT, SGT).
   - Configure secondary timezones in Google Calendar / Outlook.
2. **Scheduling Protocol:**
   - **Buffer Time:** Always leave 15 minutes buffer between back-to-back meetings.
   - **Meeting Details:** Every calendar event MUST contain:
     - Clear Title (e.g. `TechFlow Kickoff Call: Alex & Sarah`)
     - Video Conference Link (Zoom/Google Meet)
     - Brief Agenda / Goal
   - **Confirmation Email:** Send a 24-hour reminder email to all attendees with meeting agenda and link.

## SOP 2: Inbox Zero & Priority Filtering

### Objective
Process high-volume client email inboxes efficiently into actionable folders.

### Daily Workflow
1. **Morning Processing (9:00 AM):**
   - Sort emails into 4 categories:
     - `01_ACTION`: Requires client input or action today.
     - `02_WAITING`: Sent messages waiting for vendor/client response.
     - `03_READ_ARCHIVE`: Newsletters, updates, fyi.
     - `04_FINANCE`: Invoices and payment receipts.
2. **Drafting Replies:**
   - Use pre-approved canned drafts for routine inquiries.
   - For sensitive inquiries, prepare a draft in Gmail and notify the client on Slack: *"Draft ready for review regarding the Q3 proposal."*

## SOP 3: B2B Lead Generation & Lead Verification

### Objective
Extract and verify 50+ accurate prospect contact leads daily.

### Tools Needed
- Apollo.io / Hunter.io / LinkedIn Sales Navigator / NeverBounce

### Process Checklist
- [ ] **Define ICP (Ideal Customer Profile):** Job Title, Industry, Company Size, Location.
- [ ] **Extract Contact Data:** First Name, Last Name, Title, Company Name, Verified Email, LinkedIn URL.
- [ ] **Email Verification:** Run list through NeverBounce / ZeroBounce. Discard any risky or invalid emails.
- [ ] **Spreadsheet Formatting:** Format output cleanly in Google Sheets with columns: `First Name` | `Last Name` | `Title` | `Company` | `Email` | `LinkedIn` | `Status`.

---

## SOP 4: Meeting Preparation & Minute Taking

### Objective
Record actionable meeting minutes and deliver transcript summaries within 2 hours of meeting conclusion.

### Checklist
- [ ] Record meeting audio/video (Zoom/GMeet with permission).
- [ ] Note Attendance, Date, Time, and Key Topic.
- [ ] Summarize Key Decisions in bullet points.
- [ ] List **Action Items** with assigned owner and due date:
  - *Example:* `Action Item: Sarah to send updated pitch deck to Alex by Friday 5 PM EST.`
- [ ] Email Meeting Summary to all attendees within 2 hours.
